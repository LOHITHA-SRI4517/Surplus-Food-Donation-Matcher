VALIDATION & QUALITY CHECKS

FoodBridge AI — Workflow Test Cases

Verify food safety, human approval, NGO acceptance and pickup location safety before presenting the project.

TEST CASE 01

TC1 · Fresh Food & NGO Matching

Verify that fresh food passes the configured safety checks and that an eligible NGO is matched based on category, location and available capacity.

Expected result
Eligible donation is matched appropriately. Human approval is required before the pickup workflow proceeds.

Verify: Food age, safety decision, NGO match and approval status.

TEST CASE 02

TC2 · Expired Cooked Food

Verify that cooked food exceeding the configured four-hour safety window is rejected.

Expected result
Food is rejected, matching is skipped and pickup is blocked.

Verify: Food age, rejection reason and absence of an eligible pickup.

TEST CASE 03

TC3 · NGO Acceptance Pending

Verify that an NGO-pending donation cannot be marked as collected before the NGO accepts it.

Expected result
Donation remains pending. Collection is blocked until the required acceptance and pickup checks are complete.

Verify: Donation status, pickup status and collection restrictions.

TEST CASE 04

TC4 · Drop-off Location Changed

Verify that changing the restaurant's drop-off address invalidates the previous location confirmation.

Expected result
Location confirmation resets. Collection remains blocked until the NGO confirms the updated address.

Verify: Updated address, confirmation flag and pickup readiness.
